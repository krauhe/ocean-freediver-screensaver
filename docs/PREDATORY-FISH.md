# Painted comber

The small predator is a procedural approximation of *Serranus scriba*. One patrols the lower foreground at normal density; two are enabled above 72% density when graphics population quality exceeds 0.8. The Painted combers checkbox controls visibility. Hiding the seabed also hides these fish.

The species occurs in the Mediterranean and lives near rocky or Posidonia-covered bottoms. Its diet includes crustaceans and fish. Sources: [Naturalis species account](https://fishes-fnam.linnaeus.naturalis.nl/linnaeus_ng/app/views/species/nsr_taxon.php?id=109651) and [Šantić & Apostolska (2024), stomach-content study](https://ribarstvo.agr.hr/en/issues/article/1477). These support the choice of species, not the animation's exact patrol, speed or response timings. Model size is exaggerated for readability; this is not a calibrated ecosystem.

Each school fish independently assesses predator distance and approach velocity. Individual sensitivity, response delay and lateral preference produce staggered escape. Strong avoidance suppresses cohesion, then exposure and alarm decay. Fish briefly seek nearby cover before returning to their moving school. Predators track a reachable school, accelerate into a 2–3 second burst at up to 3.2 scene units/s, then retreat for 10–16 seconds. An unsuccessful approach also ends in retreat. They never remove or consume fish.

Bottom schools follow broad routes across the wreck foreground, left reef and truck exterior, roughly 2–5 units above the bottom. A third school follows the actual cliff face up and down. Route anchors move gradually and slow down when their school falls behind. The left reef has 24 irregular rock/rubble collision envelopes, using the existing static material batches. All timings, routes and shelter responses are artistic choices.

Gemini 4 Argon High supplied the model, patrol controller, avoidance changes, motor mix and initial tests through bounded text-only assignments. Codex reviewed and integrated the code, corrected interaction timestamps, pooled registration, density scaling and release discontinuities, improved stripe resolution, and replaced test stubs with the bundled Three.js implementation. No external model output is treated as biological evidence.

Checks: `node tests/predator-behavior.cjs` verifies approach/retreat response, distant-school isolation, recovery, bounded speed, pause, density, and shared hold/release. `node tests/predator-hunt.cjs` exercises repeated complete hunts in the actual terrain. `node tests/school-roaming.cjs` checks six minutes of route coverage, cliff ascent/descent and overlapping reef collision envelopes. `node tests/ambient-audio.cjs` checks local media and Web Audio gain paths. Private browser validations exercise Chrome/WebGL, the live truck/wreck geometry, visibility controls and audio-file playback.
